    The road to the platinum

    PowerPyx rates the whole list 4/10 and about 200 hours, in one playthrough, with no online trophies. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off for the whole run.

    1. Stage 1 — Story + optional missions (60–80 hours)

      Finish Chapters 1–4 and the final mission. Do the Chapter 1 meditation FIRST, the first time it is offered at Jason's house — it is the only missable step in the game. As you travel, scan vehicles and study animals through binoculars; that progress already counts toward the 100% checklist.

    2. Stage 2 — Cleanup and 100% Completion (~100 hours)

      After the story, the checklist opens at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — about 60% true completion. You need any 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 base vehicles scanned, and 100 of 255 animals studied, plus the story and the remaining checklist items. Finish the collectibles, activities and side strands you have left.

    3. Stage 3 — Strangers, miscellaneous and the platinum (~10 hours)

      Finish the 8 stranger strands, the last robberies and the vague trophies once guides have pinned them down. When the other 54 are earned, the platinum pops.

    Trophy counts: Stage 1 earns the story trophies, Stage 2 earns A Long Road and most collectibles and activities, Stage 3 earns the rest. The exact split is shown under each stage.

    Missables — only one trophy can be missed

    ⚠ MISSABLE — THE ONLY ONE

    Positive Mental Attitude

    Meditate at every spot across Leonida — 18 spots in 10 locations, as Jason and Lucia.

    The first meditation spot is offered at Jason's house in Chapter 1. It becomes permanently unavailable after that chapter, so do it the first time it is offered, before you finish Chapter 1. Every other spot can be done in free-roam after the story.

    PowerPyx and GTABase agree: this is the only potentially missable trophy in the list. Everything else is earnable in free-roam after the story ends.

    Sources: Exophase — GTA VI trophy list · PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap

    All other 54 trophies: not missable. If you miss a side mission or a collectible, go back for it in free-roam — there is no mission replay, but the open world stays open.
